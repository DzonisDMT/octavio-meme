PixelLotto

Community MiniDapp on Minima. Site in progress.

Paint a pixel. Mint an NFT. Chase the pot.

  • 24×24 grid. Each free square is a ticket.
  • Pick a colour from 12 swatches and a name, pay 1 MINIMA, and mint a 1/1 NFT, “PixelLotto #N”.
  • Fees go into a pinned pot. After 20 blocks the round ends. The winner is drawn from the block hash, and the prize goes to that winner’s Mx or 0x address.
